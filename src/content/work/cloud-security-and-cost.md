## The starting point

The platform runs on Azure and serves a large user base through many supplier integrations. Over time the monthly
cloud bill had grown faster than the business needed, and there was also compliance work to do.

## What I did

- **Cost.** Walked through the environment resource by resource, removing what was unused and moving anything
  over-provisioned to a lower tier.
- **Compliance checks.** Took failing automated tests one by one and implemented the technical change in Azure each
  one required.
- **Vulnerabilities.** Reviewed the software for known vulnerabilities and fixed what needed fixing.

- **AI tooling.** Helped put an automated development pipeline in place that uses AI assistants across the software
  lifecycle, and coached other departments on using AI tools well. AI helped locate complex bugs and potential
  vulnerabilities in hours instead of days, and every suggested change was reviewed before it was implemented.

## What changed

- Monthly Azure spend was significantly reduced.
- Compliance tests moved to a passing state.
